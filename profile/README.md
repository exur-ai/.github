## 💡 What is Exur?

Inspired by top-tier institutional risk management engines (like BlackRock's Aladdin), [Exur.ai](https://exur.ai) combines real-time data processing with specialized AI models trained on professional quantitative and financial literature.

We eliminate information asymmetry for everyday traders by unifying:
* **Live Orderbook Liquidity Analytics:** Tracking smart money flow and exchange liquidity depth.
* **Real-Time News & Sentiment Processing:** Evaluating global macroeconomic news impact in milliseconds.
* **Multi-Timeframe Technical & Fundamental Alignment:** Simultaneous analysis across multiple charts and indicators.
* **Institutional Risk Intelligence:** Automated capital protection and scenario-based risk evaluation.
